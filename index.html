Our Story

Chef Anagha's Kitchen

This isn't a restaurant. It's a kitchen run by a mother who believes every meal should feel like it was made just for you. From our home to yours, we bring the true soul of Marathi cuisine.

"Every bite feels like comfort and love, with a touch of authenticity and care that's hard to find anywhere else."

We use only premium quality rice, daal, and sunflower oil. With healthy options like jowar and bajra for patients and seniors, we never take shortcuts. Just pure, honest food.
